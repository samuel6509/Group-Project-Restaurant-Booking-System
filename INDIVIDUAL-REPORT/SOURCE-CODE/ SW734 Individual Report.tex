\section{Introduction}
\label{Introduction}
Now the project has come to an end and I have had a chance to reflect, as a whole I am pleased with how the 
project turned out and how the project progressed over this last academic year. My experience working on this 
project has been good for the most part, the tasks given to each member was completed by them (with a few 
exceptions) as well as members willing to take parts in various aspects of the project.

To briefly summarize, I believe that my contribution to this project was over what was expected of me. I was 
consistent with my contributions rather than working in smaller spurts of energy which allowed me to get tasks
done at a pace that works for me and my other group members. Although I exceeded what was expected of me, like
any person there were parts that I could have done better or been more involved in.

\section{Critical Analysis}
\label{Critical Analysis}
Although I feel like I was a strong group member throughout the duration of this project, there a parts where I
feel that there is room for improvement. These ranging from availability, holiday downtime and feature 
implementation.

\vspace*{-0.05in}
\subsection{On Myself}
\vspace*{-0.05in}
To begin, here are my top 2 parts that I am happy with myself in doing…

Firstly, I am pleased with my git contributions throughout this project. I made sure to push to git 
incrementally and regularly as I know this is a good practice not just for me but for other group members, I 
also pulled from git before I began coding for the day and pulled from git when I finished coding for the day.
This meant that I never had a problem of breaking the code base due to conflicts as I always was up to date.

Secondly, I am pleased with how I implemented my backend logic, specifically with my authentication and how 
images are loaded on the project and how users and admins can upload images to be used on the website. My 
authentication logic fully works with examples being password hashing and the use of cookies and tokens for 
logging and keeping a user logged in.

In contrast, there were parts which I done (or didn’t do) which could have been better or of a higher 
standard, with my main 3 being…

I left a one or two of my features half-baked so to speak, for example the search bar on the navigation bar. 
The way I left this was that it trims all characters to lowercase and is injected as a url, for example it only
really works with home and about as their routes are both lower case. I should of gone back and made it so like
words go to their suggested web page but I was too focused on other parts.

Another limitation I bought upon myself was having too much down time in the winter break. I did not push any 
work in these 4 weeks (roughly) which I regret doing, I done this as I didn’t want to burn out and I knew that
once term started again, I would go back to consistent work, although I stuck by my word it would have been 
better if I stayed consistent throughout the break also.

And for my last criticism, I wished I used the external pieces of software I setup, for example Trello 
workspace for the kanban board. I set this up so every group member could easily see what they needed to work 
on and what they have already done. I intended to make a new board for every sprint so members could see where
every individual was at with their feature, but we never used it in the end. Also, the weekly minutes, nearing
the end of the project we were all caught up with finishing features and I didn’t make a note of every week, I
regret doing this.

\vspace*{-0.05in}
\subsection{On The Group}
\vspace*{-0.05in}
Focusing on the group as a whole, we worked well and once we got going it was even better. Although I believe 
as a unit we worked particularly well I have some criticisms for a few of my group members, even though I 
believe they could of improved on these limiting factors as a whole everyone worked well and I am very pleased
with each and every one of my members as a whole.

My main two criticisms are these…

Oj54 was supposed to implement the menu’s as one of his features but never got round to doing it, this can be 
seen in the weekly minutes. I can commend him for doing the background research for implementing this but they
never got round to doing it. In the long run this was not an issue as I decided to take this on as one of my 
features and I am pleased with how it came out; it would have been nice for oj54 to implement this and have

Secondly, there was inconsistent participation in our weekly meeting on Friday without our supervisor. Some 
people would stay home and ask if they could join via teams, which was difficult as it meant we would have to 
find a room where we could do this without interrupting other students. The reason this is important to me was
because it allowed me to easily see what people were doing and either explain things or help members with 
issues. I understand that every person has different arrangements and issues and in the end it didn’t impact 
that the project enough to call it a serious issue.
